# GlassPanel

The two Lotus surfaces, side by side over the study: full glass for glancing, frost for reading.

- **Full glass:** `glass-tint`, `blur-glass` (with saturate 1.4), `glass-edge`, `shadow-glass`, `radius-lg` or `radius-pill`. Text in `beige`, headings in `rosy-light`.
- **Frost:** `frost-tint`, `blur-frost` (with saturate 1.3), `frost-edge`, `shadow-frost`, `radius-lg`. Text in `dark-green`, secondary in `ink-muted`.
- Always sit on the room backdrop: the study plate blurred by `blur-room`, then `room-veil`.
- The consumer provides the backdrop and the content.
