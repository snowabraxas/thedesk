# Desk identities

Same house, different rooms. Every desk follows the Lotus system's bones exactly, and turns five dials to make it unmistakably itself.

## What every desk shares (never changes)

| Fixed | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The two glass strengths, blur and shadows | One material across the house |
| Fonts, type sizes, spacing, radii | Reads as one family |
| Text colours and contrast pairs | Always readable |
| The layout skeleton: rail, top capsule, cards, glance tiles | You never have to relearn where things are |
| Wila's orb and voice in the top capsule | She's the host of every room |

## The five dials (what each desk makes its own)

| Dial | What it changes | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Its corner of the room** | The blurred backdrop is cropped to the desk's own object in the study (News sits in front of the TV, Product Feed in front of the laptop) | Same plate and time of day as the room |
| **2. A signature accent** | One colour that marks the desk: its active rail icon, progress fill and highlights | Chosen from the Lotus family (palette colours or a tint of them); never amber or yellow; used on 10% or less of the page |
| **3. One signature component** | A piece only this desk has, built from Lotus parts | Exactly one per desk |
| **4. Its own ritual and reward** | The desk's daily loop and what you earn for it | Rewards feed the shared XP and level in the room |
| **5. Motion and voice** | One small ambient motion when the desk opens, and the tone of its copy | One motion only, under 1 second, and off when reduced motion is on |

| Desk | Corner | Accent | Signature component | Ritual and reward | Motion and voice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **News** (retro TV) | The TV and low cabinet | `rosy-brown` | The ranked edition (1, 2, 3) with the breaking banner | Read all 3 to earn the day's edition stamp | A soft TV "warm-up" glow; calm, factual, newsroom voice |
| **Product Feed** (laptop) | The laptop on the desk | `moss-green` | The theme map that grows with your reads | XP, levels, the weekly ring and badges | A blinking cursor settles; curious, friendly mentor voice |
| **Astrology · Cycle Sync** (moon lamp) | The lamp and window | A lotus pink tint of `rosy-brown` | A moon-phase ring | A daily check-in streak | A slow moon glow; gentle, reflective voice |

The other desks (Scheduler, Language, Workout Coach, Games, Bookstore, Typewriter, Opportunities, Projects) get their dials at their own kickoff, chosen with Snow.

## How a new desk gets its look

1. At kickoff, Snow and the lead PM fill in the desk's five dials (one row like the ones above).
2. The designer subagent builds the page from this system plus that row, and shows Snow screenshots.
3. Nothing outside the five dials changes without updating this system first.
